# Scientific Evidence Mode

Use this mode only after `research-primary-sources` is selected and the task needs scientific hypothesis formation or study appraisal. It is not a separate global route.

## Forming hypotheses

1. Freeze the observation, population or system, measurement context, dates, and fact-versus-inference boundary.
2. State the claim type: descriptive, associational, predictive, causal, or mechanistic. Define the estimand and identification assumptions when causal.
3. Generate serious rivals, including mechanism, measurement artifact, confounding, selection or collider bias, reverse causation, stochastic variation, and context dependence where plausible.
4. Derive observations that discriminate among rivals, results that count against each one, and outcomes that remain indeterminate.
5. Match measurement and analysis to the claim; separate exploratory findings from confirmatory claims and record deviations.

## Appraising a study or evidence body

1. Separate reported observations from interpretation, causal language, mechanism claims, recommendations, and generalization.
2. Identify design, population or system, sampling, exposure or intervention, comparator, outcomes, timing, unit of observation, unit of analysis, and unit of inference.
3. Test whether design and identification assumptions can support the claim. Inspect confounding, selection, collider bias, reverse causation, contamination, controls, and rival explanations.
4. Inspect construct validity, reliability, calibration, preprocessing, exclusions, attrition, missingness, masking, and data-dependent choices.
5. Evaluate effect magnitude, uncertainty, estimand, model assumptions, multiplicity, sensitivity, prespecification, reproducibility, replication, external validity, and heterogeneity as separate dimensions.
6. Classify each material conclusion as `supported`, `qualified`, `unsupported`, or `indeterminate`, with the exact evidence or missing information responsible.

## Guardrails

- A plausible narrative is not a causal result; a rejected null or rival does not prove the preferred mechanism.
- Do not turn statistical significance, sample size, venue prestige, or an evidence hierarchy into a standalone validity verdict.
- Do not invent universal thresholds or unreported methods, data, approvals, analyses, citations, or results.
- Keep reporting completeness, internal validity, practical importance, reproducibility, and generalizability distinct.
- Do not convert appraisal into patient-specific or unsafe operational advice without separate evidence and authority.
